> Research status: owner-curated interface reference for v02 ORCS enrichment. Examples and described capabilities require confirmation for the specific programmatic retrieval contract; this note does not authorize live retrieval or define pipeline filters.


## Query fields and examples
### GEO DataSets
|Field full name|Field aliases|Description|Search term values and rules|Example|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|All Fields|ALL, *|All terms from all searchable fields. Default field.|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find any record that contains the word 'cancer'  <br>[cancer[All fields]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=cancer[All+fields])|
|Author|AUTH, AU, AUTHOR NAME|Contributors or authors associated with the study|free text, wildcard (*) supported, author initials are optional|Find records authored by A Smith  <br>[smith a[Author]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=smith+a[Author])|
|DataSet Type|GTYP, gdsType|DataSet or Series type|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find all studies that examine gene expression by high throughput sequencing  <br>[expression profiling by high throughput sequencing[DataSet Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=expression+profiling+by+high+throughput+sequencing[DataSet%20Type])|
|Description|DESC, DSC, DESCR|Text provided in the DataSet, Series or Sample description, summary and other metadata fields|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find studies that contain smoking-related terms in their descriptions  <br>[smok*[DESC]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=smok*[DESC])|
|Entry Type|ETYP, entryType|Record type|fixed list, use gds (DataSet), gse (Series) or gpl (Platform)|Find only DataSet records  <br>[gds[Entry Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=gds[Entry+Type])|
|Filter|FILT, FLTR, SUBSET, SB, FIL|Filters for records that have links to other NCBI databases|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find records that have PubMed links  <br>[gds pubmed[Filter]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=gds+pubmed[Filter])|
|GEO Accession|ACCN, accession|GEO accession number|valid DataSet (GDS), Platform (GPL), Sample (GSM) or Series (GSE) accession|Find all studies performed on Platform GPL570  <br>[GPL570[GEO Accession]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=GPL570[GEO+Accession])|
|MeSH Terms|MESH, MH, SUBH, SH, Subheading|Medical Subject Headings (MeSH) terms|[Medical Subject Headings](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/meshhome.html) (MeSH) terms, wildcard (*) supported|Find records that have MeSH term methylation  <br>[methylation[MeSH Terms]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=methylation[MeSH+Terms])|
|Number of Platform Probes|NPRO, n_probes|Number of Platform probe IDs|integer, range function supported|Find Platforms that have over 1 million probes  <br>[1000000:100000000[Number of Platform Probes]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=1000000:100000000[Number+of+Platform+Probes])|
|Number of Samples|NSAM, n_samples|Number of Samples in the DataSet or Series|integer, range function supported|Find studies with between 100 and 500 samples  <br>[100:500[Number of Samples]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=100:500[Number+of+Samples])|
|Organism|ORGN, PORGN, primary organism|Name of the organism|[NCBI taxonomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/) terms, wildcard (*) supported, all levels in the taxonomy lineage and common names are indexed|Find studies performed on mouse  <br>[Mus musculus[Organism]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=Mus+musculus[Organism])|
|Platform Technology Type|PTYP, ptechType|Platform type|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find all studies performed with next-generation sequencing technology  <br>[high throughput sequencing[Platform Technology Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=high+throughput+sequencing[Platform+Technology+Type])|
|Project|PROJ|Featured project data|fixed list, use roadmap epigenomics, encode, pilot encode, or modencode|Find studies in the NIH Roadmap Epigenomics project  <br>[roadmap epigenomics[Project]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=roadmap+epigenomics[Project])|
|Publication Date|PDAT, DP|Date on which record was released|format YYYY/MM, range function supported|Find studies published between January and June 2007  <br>[2007/01:2007/06[Publication Date]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=2007/01:2007/06[Publication+Date])|
|Related Platform|RGPL, relatedGPL|Retrieves the Plaform(s) for a specified DataSet or Series|valid DataSet (GDS) or Series (GSE) accession|Find Platforms related to GSE22474  <br>[GSE22474[Related Platform]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=GSE22474[Related+Platform])|
|Related Series|RGSE, relatedGSE|Retrieves the Series for a specified DataSet or Platform|valid DataSet (GDS) or Platform (GPL) accession|Find Series related to GPL570  <br>[GPL570[Related Series]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=GPL570[Related+Series])|
|Reporter Identifier|GEID, seqacc, clone, orf, unigene, Gene Identifier|Name or identifier of Platform probe; pertains only to Platforms that have been subjected to re-annotation pipeline|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find DataSets that include a probe corresponding to Arg1  <br>[Arg1[Reporter Identifier]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=Arg1[Reporter+Identifier])|
|Sample Source|SRC, source|The source of the biological material of the Sample; warning: submitter-supplied field, not curated|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find studies with samples from brain  <br>[brain[Sample Source]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=brain[Sample+Source])|
|Sample Type|STYP, sampType|Sample type or molecule|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find studies that use protein samples  <br>[protein[Sample Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=protein[Sample+Type])|
|Sample Value Type|VTYP, valType|Sample value type; pertains only to curated DataSets|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find DataSets with log ratio sample values  <br>[log ratio[Sample Value Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=log+ratio[Sample+Value+Type])|
|Submitter Institute|INST, institute|Institute or organization as given in submitter account|free text|Find data submitted by the Broad Institute  <br>[Broad Institute[Institute]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=Broad+Institute[Institute])|
|Subset Description|SSDE, SSDESC|DataSet subset descriptions|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find DataSets that include the term 'male' in subset description  <br>[male[Subset Description]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=male[Subset+Description])|
|Subset Variable Type|SSTP, SSTYPE|Name of DataSet experimental variable|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find DataSets that have 'age' as an experimental variable  <br>[age[Subset Variable Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=age[Subset+Variable+Type])|
|Supplementary Files|SFIL, SFILE, suppFile|Supplementary file type names|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find studies that have Affymetrix CEL files  <br>[cel[Supplementary Files]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=cel[Supplementary+Files])|
|Tag Length|TAGL, taglength|SAGE or MPSS tag length in base pairs|integer|Find 10 base pair SAGE data  <br>[10[Tag Length]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=10[Tag+Length])|
|Title|TITL, TITLE, TI|Text from titles of DataSets, Series, Platforms, and Samples|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find records where 'Affymetrix' appears in a title  <br>[Affymetrix[Title]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=Affymetrix[Title])|
|Update Date|UDAT|Date on which record was last updated|format YYYY/MM, range function supported|Find records updated during June 2010  <br>[2010/06[Update Date]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gds?term=2010/06[Update+Date])|

### GEO Profiles

|Field full name|Field aliases|Description|Search term values and rules|Example|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|All Fields|ALL, *|All terms from all searchable fields. Default field.|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find P450 genes in DataSets that investigate smoking  <br>[smok* AND P450](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=smok*+AND+P450)|
|Annotation Type|ATYP, annot_type|Source of annotation|fixed list, use gene, nucleotide, unigene or protein|Find profiles with Gene-based annotation  <br>[gene[Annotation Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=gene[Annotation+Type])|
|Base Position|CPOS, CPOSITION, CHRPOS|Base pair position on chromosome|integer, range function supported, must be used in conjuction with Chromosome field|Find profiles that lie between base positions 10000 to 3000000 on chromosome 8 in mouse  <br>[(8[Chromosome] AND 10000:3000000[Base Position]) AND mouse[organism]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=\(8[Chromosome]+AND+10000:3000000[Base+Position]\)+AND+mouse[organism])|
|Chromosome|CHR, CHROMOSOME, CH, CHROM|Chromosome number or name|chromosome number or name|Find profiles that lie between base positions 10000 to 3000000 on chromosome 8 in mouse  <br>[(8[Chromosome] AND 10000:3000000[Base Position]) AND mouse[organism]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=\(8[Chromosome]+AND+10000:3000000[Base+Position]\)+AND+mouse[organism])|
|DataSet Type|GTYP, gdsType|DataSet type|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find MPSS profiles  <br>[expression profiling by mpss[DataSet Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=expression+profiling+by+mpss[DataSet+Type])|
|Filter|FILT, FLTR, SUBSET, SB, FIL|Filters for records that have links to other NCBI databases|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles/advanced/) Preview/Index page for list of indexed terms|Find profiles that have links to NCBI's Gene database  <br>[geo gene[Filter]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=geo+gene[Filter])|
|Flag Information|FINF, FLAG_INFO, NOTE|Profiles of specific _subset types_ and for which a _subset effect_ is found. GEO DataSets are partitioned into subsets that reflect experimental design. Profiles are flagged as having subset effects if they display differential expression across experimental variables. CAUTION: The subset effect scoring method is ad hoc, taking into account group medians, means, deviation inside the groups, penalties and arbitrary cutoff thresholds. This flag is simply an attempt to give potentially differentially-regulated genes higher visibility, and is not intended to provide an absolute determination of significance.|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find profiles that exhibit subset effects with respect to age or development stage  <br>[age[Flag Information] OR development stage[Flag Information]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=age[Flag+Information]+OR+development+stage[Flag+Information])|
|Flag Type|FTYP, FLAG_TYPE|Profiles that exhibit specific types of _subset effects_. GEO DataSets are partitioned into subsets that reflect experimental design. Profiles are flagged as having subset effects if they display differential expression across experimental variables. CAUTION: The subset effect scoring method is ad hoc, taking into account group medians, means, deviation inside the groups, penalties and arbitrary cutoff thresholds. This flag is simply an attempt to give potentially differentially-regulated genes higher visibility, and is not intended to provide an absolute determination of significance.|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles/advanced/) for list of indexed terms|Find profiles that exhibit rank subset effects  <br>[rank subset effect[Flag Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=rank+subset+effect[Flag+Type])|
|GDS Text|GDST, GDStxt|Text from DataSet title and summary|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find profiles for Datasets that investigate muscular dystrophy  <br>[muscular dystrophy[GDS Text]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=muscular+dystrophy[GDS+Text])|
|GEO Accession|ACCN, accession|GEO accession number|valid DataSet (GDS), Platform (GPL), Sample (GSM) or Series (GSE) accession|Find profiles for Platform GPL570  <br>[GPL570[GEO Accession]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=GPL570[GEO+Accession])|
|GEO Description/Title Text|GEOT, TI, GEOtxt|Text provided in the DataSet or Series description, title and other metadata fields|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find profiles from studies that examine aspirin  <br>[aspirin[GEO Description/Title Text]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=aspirin[GEO%20Description/Title+Text])|
|GI|GI|Mapped GenBank Identifier|integer|Find profiles for GenBank Identifier 89145416  <br>[89145416[GI]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=89145416[GI])|
|Gene Description|GDSC, GEND, aliases, GENE, GeneDesc|Gene description and aliases from Gene, title from UniGene.|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find kinase genes in GDS182  <br>[kinase[Gene Description] AND GDS182](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=kinase[Gene+Description]+AND+GDS182)|
|Gene Ontology|GO|Gene Ontology terms|[Gene Ontology](http://www.ebi.ac.uk/GOA/) (GO) terms, wildcard (*) supported|Find apoptosis genes in GDS182  <br>[apoptosis[Gene Ontology] AND GDS182](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=apoptosis[Gene%20Ontology]+AND+GDS182)|
|Gene Symbol|SYMB, GeneSymbol|Gene Symbol from Gene or UniGene|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find CYP1A1 gene  <br>[CYP1A1[Gene Symbol]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=CYP1A1[Gene%20Symbol])|
|ID_REF|ID, ID_REF|ID from GEO Platform, SAGE tag, Affy ProbeSet ID|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find profiles for Affymetrix probeset ID 218973_at  <br>[218973_at[ID_REF]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=218973_at[ID_REF])|
|Max Value Rank|RMAX, RNKMX|The maximum value percentile rank for any Sample within DataSet|integer, 0-100, range function supported|Find profiles where the maximum rank percentile is in the 1st percentile (ie, genes with low expression)  <br>[1[Max Value Rank]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=1[Max+Value+Rank])|
|Min Value Rank|RMIN, RNKMN|The minimum value percentile rank for any Sample within DataSet|integer, 0-100, range function supported|Find profiles where the minimum rank percentile is in the 100th percentile (ie, highly expressed genes)  <br>[100[Min Value Rank]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=100[Min+Value+Rank])|
|Number of Samples|NSAM, n_samples|Number of Samples in the DataSet|integer, range function supported|Find profiles with between 100 and 200 samples  <br>[100:200[Number of Samples]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=100:200[Number+of+Samples])|
|Organism|ORGN|Name of the organism|[NCBI taxonomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/) terms, wildcard (*) supported, all levels in the taxonomy lineage and common names are indexed|Find mouse profiles  <br>[Mus musculus[Organism]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=Mus+musculus[Organism])|
|Platform Reporter Type|RTYP, rep_type|Platform reporter type used for annotation|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find profiles where a CLONE ID is the basis for annotation  <br>[Mus musculus[Organism]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=Mus+musculus[Organism])|
|Ranked Standard Deviation|RSTD, RNSTD|Percentile rank of profile standard deviation compared to all other profiles in a DataSet|integer, 0-100, range function supported|Find profiles with a high level of standard deviation  <br>[100[Ranked Standard Deviation]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=100[Ranked+Standard+Deviation])|
|Reporter Identifier|NAME, identifier, Gene Identifier|Name or identifier of Platform probe|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find profiles that include a probe corresponding to Arg1  <br>[D00636[Reporter Identifier]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=D00636[Reporter+Identifier])|
|Sample Source|SRC, source|The source of the biological material of the Sample; warning: submitter-supplied field, not curated|free text, wildcard (*) supported|Find profiles with samples from brain  <br>[brain[Sample Source]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=brain[Sample+Source])|
|Sample Value Type|VTYP, value_type|Sample value type|fixed list, check [Advanced Search](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles/advanced/) page for list of indexed terms|Find profiles with log ratio sample values  <br>[log ratio[Sample Value Type]](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geoprofiles?term=log+ratio[Sample+Value+Type])|
