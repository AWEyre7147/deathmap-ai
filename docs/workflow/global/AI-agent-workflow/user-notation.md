Status: proposed, not adopted. The owner will establish the workflow before
starting the enrichment session. These prefixes are examples for future use;
ordinary language remains sufficient to express current instructions.

|    Prefix     |               Meaning               |               AI-agent response                |
| :-----------: | :---------------------------------: | :--------------------------------------------: |
| **Explore:**  | Think aloud, compare possibilities  |           Discuss; do not implement            |
| **Remember:** |     Preserve an idea for later      | Record in an ideas note, explicitly unapproved |
|  **Decide:**  | Establish a requirement or boundary |  Record the decision; identify any conflicts   |
|    **Do:**    |     Authorize a specific action     | Carry out that action within its stated scope  |

Respect explicit Explore, Remember, Decide, and Do labels. Do not require labels when ordinary language is clear.

Do not turn speculative language, brainstorming, or assistant suggestions into implementation requirements. Ask only when ambiguity would materially affect scope or behavior.

Keep accepted decisions separate from unapproved ideas. When recording either, preserve its status, source point, rationale, and relevant scope.

A decision establishes direction; an action request authorizes work. Apply existing authorizations without repeatedly asking for permission.

Explain consequential assumptions and offer concrete examples of their effects. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing agreement or completeness.
